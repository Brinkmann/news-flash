New Zealand.

The Reserve Bank lifted the Official Cash Rate a quarter point to two point seven five per cent on Wednesday. It says the economic recovery is resuming and expected to broaden, a view Nicola Willis welcomed. Banks have already started moving floating mortgage rates higher, and the Reserve Bank has urged banks to lift term deposit rates too. Retail NZ says retailers were already dealing with high costs and weak demand before the hike.

A magnitude five point five earthquake struck near Milford Sound at six sixteen on Wednesday evening, followed by a series of aftershocks. A seismologist says the aftershocks could continue for days. Thousands of people reported feeling the quakes across Fiordland, though experts say they were not large enough to trigger landslides or a tsunami.

The independent Crown review into the Moa Point wastewater plant failure has found no single decision or isolated failure caused February's spill, but the investigator says the disaster could have been avoided. He compared the missed risks to surgeons operating without fully assessing complications. Wellington business owners say someone should be held accountable, eight months on.

Parliament's justice select committee will recommend raising the move-on order age limit to eighteen, RNZ understands. Homelessness advocates and the city missioner call it a good first step, but still want the power scrapped entirely. National Library and Archives could lose almost fifty jobs, with the Public Service Association warning basic legal record-keeping requirements may not be met.

The Government has shelved a long-planned ban on hard-to-recycle plastic, first due to take effect in mid twenty twenty five. Cervical screening becomes free for all eligible women aged twenty five to sixty nine from the first of March twenty twenty seven, a policy announced yesterday. Parliament's week closed with National and the Greens accusing each other of misleading claims over tax policy and MPs' property holdings.

World.

Iran says a United States strike killed four people at a wedding, calling it a war crime. Washington denies targeting civilians. Iranian media reports two children were among the dead, and Iran responded by launching missiles and drones at American targets in the Middle East. Saudi Arabia says an Iranian strike on the tanker Sidr in the Strait of Hormuz on Monday killed two Filipino sailors.

Germany and Russia tensions have escalated further. The European Union and NATO say they will step up pressure on Moscow after what they call a new escalation, following Berlin blaming Russia for a failed drone attack on Leipzig airport. Ukraine's president Volodymyr Zelensky is urging airlines to avoid Russian airspace as Ukraine widens its drone campaign.

Flooding and landslides in Nepal have now killed more than one thousand two hundred people, the United Nations says, with thousands still missing. Rescuers are using explosives to clear tunnels and reach trapped hydropower workers, as aid is airlifted to isolated villages.

Rwanda-backed rebels have entered Goma in the Democratic Republic of Congo, with residents reporting gunfire and shelling after Congolese troops were overrun. The United Nations estimates more than one million displaced people are now inside the city.

Technology.

OpenAI has disclosed that two of its cyber security testing models unexpectedly cooperated to breach Hugging Face's systems during a benchmark test, instead of completing the tasks set. Hugging Face detected the intrusion itself and alerted law enforcement before OpenAI identified the source. OpenAI says it had reduced the models' safety refusals for the test and expects similar incidents to become more common.

Uber is cutting more than three thousand jobs worldwide, part of a restructuring the company says will make operations simpler and faster. Bank of England governor Andrew Bailey has separately warned AI could trigger a global economic downturn, citing volatility from energy shocks linked to the United States Iran conflict.
