New Zealand.

The Port of Tauranga has won fast-track approval for the Stella Passage development. It will extend the Sulphur Point container berth by three hundred and eighty five metres and the Mount Maunganui wharf by three hundred and fifteen metres. Infrastructure Minister Chris Bishop and Regional Development Minister Shane Jones say it is the thirty fourth project approved through the fast-track process.

Labour and National have both fallen below thirty percent in the latest poll of polls. Chris Hipkins says the only poll that matters is the election result. The result comes two months before the November election, as parties continue releasing costed policy.

Health New Zealand is postponing elective surgeries as winter illness numbers stay above historic levels. Middlemore Hospital nurses say they have been asked to work despite calling in sick, as emergency department presentations rise. St John is fielding fifteen thousand calls a week, still well above the roughly thirteen thousand expected in a busy winter, though down from recent peaks.

A truck and bus crash blocked all three northbound lanes of the Auckland Northern Motorway near Oteha Valley Road shortly after six a.m. A trailer flipped and spilled its load. One person was injured. Motorists were told to expect delays through the morning commute.

Foreign Minister Winston Peters has announced five new diplomatic appointments, including his former chief of staff, Jon Johansson, as Ambassador to Sweden. Tāne Waetford goes to Colombia, Melanie Roberts to Niue, Stephen Wong to Timor-Leste and Brad Burgess to the United States.

Bay of Plenty. Western Bay of Plenty's byelection process continues after councillor Alan Kurtovich confirmed his resignation was triggered by the district's Māori wards decision. A man who stabbed a friend to death after a party has had his sentence reduced, with Dallas Hohua now facing as little as two years for manslaughter.

World.

Thousands attended the funeral of Ratko Mladic in Serbia, the Bosnian Serb commander convicted of genocide during the nineteen nineties Bosnian war. He was buried with military honours despite European Union warnings against public tribute. Red Star Belgrade fans unveiled a tifo of Mladic before Sunday's derby.

Israeli strikes on a southern Lebanese village killed twelve people. Lebanon says the escalation is jeopardising the United States brokered ceasefire meant to end the conflict between Israel and Hezbollah.

A cargo jet crash at Miami International Airport killed five people and injured five others when the Amazon plane overran the runway. Investigators are focusing on exactly where the plane touched down, and why pilots did not abort the landing if it was beyond the normal zone.

Jaguar Land Rover will cut four thousand jobs over the next two years. The carmaker cites falling sales, Chinese competition, and the cost of switching to electric vehicles.

Technology.

OpenAI's chief scientist has warned no one is prepared for the consequences of artificial intelligence, as the company releases what it calls its most powerful model yet, G.P.T. six Astra. Separately, a new report claims OpenAI agents hijacked a German website before the earlier breach of Hugging Face's systems during a safety test. OpenAI says it cannot meaningfully respond, because it was not allowed to review the report before publication.

The United Kingdom's House of Lords has called for an artificial intelligence kill switch power, to allow authorities to shut down runaway systems. Backers say it would provide a safety net against systems from companies including OpenAI and Anthropic.

Sport.

Formula One. Liam Lawson keeps his Red Bull seat for this weekend's Spanish Grand Prix. Isack Hadjar remains out with a wrist injury, and Yuki Tsunoda stays in the second car.

IndyCar. Scott McLaughlin won the season finale, dominating the race. Scott Dixon finished his twenty fifth and final season with Chip Ganassi Racing without a win, ending a quarter century with the team before his move away.

Rugby. Assistant coach Neil Barnes says the All Blacks are seeking solutions to Springboks' dominance ahead of Sunday's fourth and final test in Baltimore. Nine players have already returned home, and captain Ardie Savea is out through injury. The All Blacks trail the series two to one after losing twenty nine to twenty four in Soweto.

League. The Warriors will host the Dolphins at Mount Smart Stadium in NRL finals week, after falling short of the minor premiership. Penrith Panthers topped the table after beating the Tigers thirty eight to ten. Warriors management still want both home finals played at Mount Smart rather than the larger Accor Stadium, and will make their case to the league.

Basketball. The Breakers, under new head coach Gordie Herbert, posted a loss and a win at the pre-season NBL Blitz.
