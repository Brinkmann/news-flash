The All Blacks have suffered their worst ever defeat to England, losing thirty seven to twelve at Twickenham, a week after their shock loss to Wales.

New Zealand.

The All Blacks were beaten in every facet at Twickenham, losing thirty seven to twelve. It is England's first back to back win over New Zealand since two thousand three. Coach Dave Rennie said the side were miles off the pace. New Zealand Rugby faces renewed pressure over Rennie's position after back to back losses to Wales and England.

The High Court's ruling that the government breached women's rights over pay equity changes continues to reverberate. The coalition scrapped dozens of claims and made new ones harder to lodge, and a judge found that discriminated against women and breached the Bill of Rights Act. A former National MP says Parliament must now deal with the issue on two fronts, through new legislation and through the claims themselves. Workers' groups have welcomed the ruling.

NZ First list MP Michael Laws is refusing calls to quit Parliament after mocking an Opportunity Party opponent's appearance and making a sexual remark about her. He says he will resist what he calls tyranny, and Winston Peters has not forced him out.

Serious injuries after a car hit a power pole on State Highway three near Ōhaupō, south of Hamilton, about three ten this morning. Police closed the road between Airport Road and Kaipaki Road.

In the Bay of Plenty, Western Bay of Plenty district councillor Alan Kurtovich has quit over the council's Māori wards decision, triggering a byelection. He says the decision to resign, despite the cost of a byelection, was the right one.

A man who stabbed a friend to death after a party has had his sentence reduced. Dallas Hohua may now serve as little as two years for the manslaughter.

World.

Rwanda backed rebels have entered Goma in the Democratic Republic of Congo. Residents report gunfire and shelling, and Congolese troops have been overrun. The United Nations estimates more than one million displaced people are now inside the city.

A Mexican drug lord on the FBI's ten most wanted list has been arrested after a shootout. Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, known as El Chapo Isidro, was detained in Sinaloa state after gunfire and burning trucks as Mexican forces clashed with cartel gunmen.

Fighting continues over fossil fuels at the COP thirty one climate talks, with nearly two hundred countries deadlocked on a new global agreement. Vanuatu's climate minister Ralph Regenvanu says fossil fuels must be phased out for Pacific nations to survive.

Ukraine's president Zelensky says strikes on the Crimean Bridge were a fair response to Russia, after more than four hundred drones and one hundred and ten missiles hit Ukraine overnight, killing at least seven in Kyiv. UK defence secretary Wes Streeting, meeting Zelensky in Kyiv, called Vladimir Putin a cold blooded killer.

Technology.

Anthropic has become one of the world's most valuable public companies after raising eighty one billion US dollars in its stock market debut, the first purely AI company to list. But its shares then slid as part of a wider tech sell off that pushed the S&P five hundred and Dow Jones into correction territory. BBC analysis questions whether the float can ease fears of a broader AI investment bubble.

RNZ reports that autonomous AI agents can now breach a system in as little as fifteen minutes, where a human hacker might take nine days. Experts say a rise in attackers using the open source tool Artex over the past week means New Zealand organisations must act. Separately, Bookit, Booking.com and Expedia have each confirmed data breaches in recent months, and RNZ urges caution when booking travel.

Sport.

Rugby. Ireland beat the Springboks twenty to seventeen in Dublin, but South Africa still makes the Nations Championship final on points differential over New Zealand. France lead Argentina in Paris, and Wales host Australia, both Nations Championship tests still in progress.

Cricket. Black Caps versus India, first test at the Basin Reserve. India lead by more than two hundred runs with nine wickets in hand going into day three, after New Zealand centuries from Henry Nicholls and Glenn Phillips. Duffy struck late for the Black Caps.

Mixed martial arts. New Zealander Navajo Stirling knocked out former champion Jiří Procházka at UFC Qatar. A win over Procházka strengthens his case for a title shot against his own teammate, Carlos Ulberg, the current champion.

Athletics. Rotorua's Hannah Gapes has won the NCAA Division One cross country title, setting a course record. She is only the second New Zealander to win the crown.

Football. Wellington Phoenix beat Western Sydney Wanderers two nil for a third straight A-League win under coach Bev Priestman.
