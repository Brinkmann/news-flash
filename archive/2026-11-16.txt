Microsoft says Outlook, Teams and Copilot are now fully restored after last week's global outage, which hit the National Health Service, schools, police forces and governments from the United States to Australia. Technology.

A survey of more than four hundred New Zealand business leaders found nearly half have been hit by a cyber incident, but only forty four per cent have a resilience plan, and just sixteen per cent of those test it regularly. The finding lands as officials call cyber protection of critical infrastructure a priority, though they say that work will take at least a year following last week's Microsoft outage.

A survey by primary health organisation ProCare has found more New Zealand GP practices using artificial intelligence than expected, alongside safety and privacy concerns. Separately, Victoria University lecturers are steering students away from AI use, bringing back in-person testing and oral exams to rethink assessment in response.
