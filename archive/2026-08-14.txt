New Zealand.

Christopher Luxon says he has total confidence no one else was involved in Chris Penk's failed leadership challenge, but won't release the vote count from Wednesday's caucus meeting. Newsroom reports National is reeling from the week's chaos, with a new poll of polls showing the coalition's chance of retaining government falling while the Opportunity Party's support rises. Parliament sits again this week.

New Zealand's Security Intelligence Service says the country faces its most challenging security environment in recent times. It warns foreign states are increasingly seeking policy insights, intellectual property and data sets from New Zealand institutions. This follows Tuesday's separate warning about a Chinese space tracking bid and covert political interference ahead of November's election.

Police shot and killed an armed man outside Sylvia Park shopping centre in Auckland on Thursday. The man had stolen an air rifle from a Penrose gun store and was approached by officers as he neared the mall. Two investigations have now been launched into the shooting.

A man has been charged with threatening to kill National MP Dan Bidois in a social media message. He faces up to seven years in jail if convicted. The case comes as the SIS flags rising harassment risks facing candidates ahead of the election.

Gisborne residents are being told to clear gutters and drains and avoid low-lying areas as heavy rain moves through. MetService warns of frost spreading north behind the system as colder weather follows.

In the Bay of Plenty, the byelection triggered by Alan Kurtovich's resignation over Western Bay of Plenty's Māori wards decision continues, with council confirming the vote was the direct cause of the seat becoming vacant. Kurtovich says the decision was worth the cost.

World.

The World Health Organisation's director general says the current Ebola outbreak is on track to become the deadliest ever recorded. More than two thousand people have died since it was declared on the fifteenth of May, with thousands more cases reported. Containment efforts continue in the affected region.

Israeli troops have been telling Palestinian families to leave their homes in the village of Qusra, according to the mayor, with soldiers reportedly using some houses as barracks after settlers besieged the village. The situation remains unresolved.

United States annual inflation eased to three point four percent in July, with food costs slowing and housing the main upward pressure remaining. It follows easing seen the previous month.

Romania has shut down its only nuclear power plant after heat caused a huge drop in the Danube River's level. The Cernavodă plant normally supplies twenty percent of the country's electricity and is not expected to restart within ten days.
