New Zealand's defence chief has resigned over an inappropriate relationship, and the country's fire service boss remains on leave after losing his minister's confidence.

New Zealand.

Air Marshal Tony Davies has resigned as Chief of Defence over what the Defence Minister called an inappropriate relationship. Judith Collins said the circumstances made his position no longer tenable. Reported by RNZ and Newsroom. No replacement has been named yet.

Fire and Emergency chief executive Kerry Gregory has resigned, effective immediately, Fire and Emergency confirmed on Friday. Internal Affairs Minister Brooke van Velden had publicly withdrawn confidence in him in September, accusing him of withholding information for a year. He had been on leave since. Reported by RNZ and Newsroom.

Annual inflation came in at three point seven percent to September, a matter of official record. Petrol price falls in July and August drove the dip. Economists expect prices to rise again soon, and there are strong odds the Reserve Bank lifts the Official Cash Rate next week, likely by twenty five basis points. Oil prices have hit their highest level since May on reports the United States is again weighing strikes on Iran, which could push fuel costs back up here.

Labour candidate Kerrin Leoni has broken her silence after resigning over a photo showing her with family members and gang associates at a function. She says her whānau has been turned into a political weapon and the criticism is being used for political gain. Chris Hipkins called her conduct unacceptable when she quit. Reported by RNZ and Newsroom, with Newsroom suggesting more Labour resignations could follow before polling day on November the seventh.

TVNZ staff will strike for the first time in twenty years over pay, after the state broadcaster offered a two and a half percent rise against higher inflation, according to the E tū union. No strike date has been confirmed. Separately, around three hundred Ikea staff in Auckland will walk off the job on Saturday over weekend penalty rates.

In Tauranga, the Western Bay of Plenty byelection continues, triggered by Alan Kurtovich's resignation over the district's Māori wards decision. Kurtovich says the decision was absolutely the right one despite the cost to him.

World.

Gunmen opened fire inside a church in Jordan during a funeral on Friday, killing at least seven people and injuring more than thirty, officials say. A manhunt is under way. Reported by BBC and Stuff via wire copy.

A critical flaw in the widely used PHP programming language has triggered a global cyber security scramble, now in its second day, with researchers saying artificial intelligence helped uncover vulnerabilities described as among the most significant ever found. The UK government says it has found five hundred new vulnerabilities in its own systems. Companies worldwide are still racing to patch. Reported by BBC Technology.

Rwanda backed M23 rebels have entered the Congolese city of Goma, with residents reporting ongoing gunfire and shelling. The United Nations estimates more than one million displaced people are now inside the city. Reported by the Wall Street Journal, continuing from yesterday.

A deadly shooting near Paris has been called a racist terrorist attack by President Macron. One person was killed and five wounded, and the suspected gunman has been apprehended. Reported by BBC.

Technology.

Elon Musk has confirmed he is considering merging Tesla and SpaceX, saying special committees at both companies are examining the idea. BBC reports it would be the biggest corporate merger in history if it proceeds. No timeline has been given.

Barclays is cutting five thousand jobs, a five percent reduction in its workforce, as it increases its reliance on artificial intelligence, the bank says. Reported by BBC.

Sport.

Formula One. United States Grand Prix practice in Austin. Charles Leclerc fastest ahead of Max Verstappen in first practice, a matter of record from BBC Sport. Liam Lawson was tenth fastest for Racing Bulls after a major upgrade package, though his best lap was later deleted. Reported by Stuff and NZ Herald. Qualifying and the race are still to come this weekend New Zealand time.

Rugby League World Cup. Tonga beat Lebanon thirty six to nil in Sydney for their second win of the tournament. Samoa thrashed Wales, scoring fourteen tries in a seventy six to nil win, their first victory of the pool stage. Both results are matters of record from RNZ and BBC Sport.

Cricket. Glenn Phillips top scored but could not prevent India beating the Black Caps in the T20 opener, India's highest ever successful run chase against New Zealand. Captain Mitchell Santner says preparation gives him confidence for the rest of the series. The White Ferns' Rose Bowl hopes were rained out, with Australia already retaining the trophy.

League. Warriors hooker Wayde Egan was spotted in New Zealand Kiwis kit the same day he extended his Auckland club contract, fuelling speculation over a Rugby League World Cup recall, reported by RNZ. The Australian Rugby League has confirmed it will keep the PNG Chiefs name for its nineteenth NRL franchise, despite a legal challenge from New Zealand Rugby. The franchise unveiled its logo and jersey in Port Moresby ahead of a twenty twenty eight debut.

Rugby. Northland say winning Monday's NPC final against Canterbury in Christchurch would rank as their greatest achievement. They have already beaten Canterbury once this season.

Tennis. Rising player Alexandra Eala, inside the world's top twenty, has confirmed she will play the ASB Classic in Auckland this summer, reported by RNZ.
