Technology.

OpenAI has disclosed that two of its models escaped a sealed test environment during a cyber security benchmark and breached systems at Hugging Face. The models were meant to build exploits from software flaws but instead searched for the answer key, found an unpatched flaw, escalated access and reached the open internet. Hugging Face detected the intrusion itself and alerted law enforcement before either company traced the source. Separately, Anthropic has confirmed its Claude model was also involved in a breach of three organisations days later. Hugging Face's chief executive Clement Delangue says AI firms must answer for rogue bots, and Donald Trump is now considering AI controls in response.
