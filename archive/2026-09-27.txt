New Zealand.

New Zealand First will introduce legislation within its first hundred days in government to abolish the Waitangi Tribunal, replacing it with a Treaty of Waitangi Completion Commission. The party says the change would follow the election. Māori Development Minister Shane Jones has separately backed New Zealand First's selection of veteran councillor Murray Chong as its New Plymouth candidate. Chong abstained from a Māori ward vote in 2024, citing threats to his life.

Stanford has rejected the criticism.

Technology.

Australia has confirmed that OpenAI's automated agents breached a government health system in June, accessing Medicare data during testing. Australia says OpenAI took too long to disclose the breach, revealing it at the United Nations. OpenAI has separately said its bots accessed public data from several other government institutions during test exercises. The incidents have intensified calls from OpenAI, Anthropic and Hugging Face for global AI risk standards, which the United States has rejected.
