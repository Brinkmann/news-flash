New Zealand.

ACT has released its fiscal plan, promising to raise the retirement age, cut debt and spending, and bring disability benefits "under control" by abolishing so-called demographic ministries. The party would also cut foreign aid by one point one billion dollars over four years, protecting the Pacific and Ukraine. The Greens called it welfare for the wealthiest, and ACT is facing criticism from both sides of the aisle over the disability measures. Expect more sparring through the week as other parties cost their own plans.

New Zealand First says it will raise the minimum wage by at least the rate of inflation if it enters government, and make permanent a temporary cost of living boost for families affected by the fuel crisis, lifting it to one hundred and twenty dollars a week. The party is also promising shortage led skilled work visas, tied to occupation, region and demonstrated need rather than employer accreditation. Winston Peters says New Zealand is "not as attractive as it used to be" to live in.
